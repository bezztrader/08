# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS + Motion (motion.dev), pinned by the user's brief. Mobile-first. Hosting not decided (assumed Vercel).

## Users

Men and boys from Freguesia / Jacarepaguá (Rio de Janeiro), mostly arriving from Instagram on a phone, deciding whether to book a cut or beard and wanting the fastest path to a WhatsApp booking. Secondary: regulars considering the monthly subscription (Clube Zero Oito).

## Product Purpose

One-page site for Barbearia Zero Oito ("Barbearia 08"). It shows the experience and the work, lists services and the Clube, gives hours and location, and turns a visit into a WhatsApp booking.

## Positioning

A premium neighborhood barbershop: "Experiência além do corte". The experience is the differentiator: precise finishing, hot towel and steam, straight-razor beard ritual, hair wash to finish, a space designed in detail.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- All copy in Brazilian Portuguese; confident, close, premium-neighborhood tone; no marketing clichés.
- The site presents the place: no prices, amounts or promotions anywhere (user decision, 2026-11-13). Details go through WhatsApp.
- Services: corte, barba, corte + barba, acabamento/pezinho, lavagem, ritual de barba com toalha quente.
- Clube Zero Oito: monthly subscription with several services included; presented without amounts.
- Photos are small (360-640px); never stretched full-screen. Videos are 720x1280 portrait.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: shield with gold varsity "08", bone pinstripe field, black ribbons "BARBEARIA" / "ZERO OITO", crossed razors, a star. File: public/images/logo-zerooito.png (transparent).
- Palette: deep black, aged gold (sparingly), bone white. Banned: purple, neon gradients, Inter.
- Type: varsity/slab condensed headlines; clean sans body.
- Brand details: subtle pinstripes, giant outlined "08", the logo star as icon/divider.
- Existing flyers (public/images/informativos) show the brand's black + gold + condensed type; reference only, never shown on the site.

## Evidence on Hand

- 4 photos in public/images/cortes (kid fade, barber at work, aligned beard, hair wash).
- 3 portrait videos with posters in public/videos (experience, salon + fade finish, beard ritual).
- No testimonials, reviews or founding year supplied. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The experience is the proof: show the ritual, not adjectives.
2. One action: every path ends at WhatsApp.
3. Phone first, Instagram arrivals.
4. Never upscale small media; frame it at its native size.
5. No prices or promotions on the site.
